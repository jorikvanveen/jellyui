The SVGs in `filled-lucide/` come from [Filled Lucide](https://filledlucide.dev/), revision [3240f4a01994](https://github.com/linus-sch/filled-lucide/tree/3240f4a01994dcbe7c2504c2cf1d9a3210573ce1/icons). They are unmodified upstream files.

The license and original Lucide notices are retained in `licenses/FILLED-LUCIDE.txt`. GPUI colors the icons using the app theme.

`disc-3` and `users-round` label the Albums and Artists navigation buttons. `volume-2` and `volume-x` show the player volume state.

`play` and `list-plus` label the Play and Queue buttons over album covers.
